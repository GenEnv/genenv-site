AI4DM

Teaching AI beyond the render.

AI4DM is a teaching platform for design representation in the age of generative AI. It begins from the premise that design media do more than visualize finished ideas: they shape how designers observe, reason, test, and communicate. While image models often reduce representation to photorealistic rendering, AI4DM reconnects emerging AI workflows with a broader culture of sketches, measured drawings, diagrams, maps, models, collages, and visual narratives.

Approach

The platform brings together disciplinary precedents, practical methods, design transformations, and guided experimentation. Rather than treating AI as a shortcut to polished images, it helps students define constraints, preserve design intent, work iteratively, and combine generated material with manual and computational techniques.

Where it runs

AI4DM is currently being developed as a shared teaching resource for ARCH 515 Design Media I and DES 212 Design Media II at UBC SALA. It continues an earlier exploration of AI-assisted design representation initiated through landscapearchitecture.ai, an open-source tutorial platform for generative AI in landscape visualization and analysis.
