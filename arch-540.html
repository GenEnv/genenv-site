ARCH 540 Designing the Design

Building custom AI tools for architectural workflows.

The studio’s position is that the object of design is no longer only the building; it is also the workflow that produces, checks, and acts on design decisions. Students do not deliver a building proposal. They design, build, and govern working tools that make architectural knowledge operational, each grounded in a real workflow, fully documented, and shared as openly as its context allows.

Phase 1 · Learning to work with AI

The first month is learned by making. Students describe the tool they want, test what the AI agent builds, and revise, building four capabilities: Build, Connect, Ground, and Automate. In Assignment 1, Making Design Knowledge Interactive, each student takes one piece of a Vancouver regulation or design guideline and builds an interactive tool that explains, computes, and visualizes it. Together the studio produces the Vancouver Regulation Toolkit.

Phase 2 · Applied practice project

Working individually or in pairs, students develop a working tool around a real workflow in architecture or landscape architecture. The project begins not with an idea for a tool but with a careful study of a process, and moves through four stages: Investigate, Build, Harden, and Deliver. Every tool runs live at the final review.
