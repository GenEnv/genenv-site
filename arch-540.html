ARCH 540 Designing the Design

What should AI do for architecture?

ARCH 540 Designing the Design investigates how artificial intelligence might reshape architectural practice by shifting attention from generated artifacts to the systems through which design work is organized. Rather than producing a conventional building proposal, students treat workflows, methods, and tools as objects of design.

Three stages

The studio progresses through three stages: experimentation, investigation, and prototyping. Students test emerging AI techniques, map a specific professional workflow, and identify its assumptions, stakeholders, and points of friction. They then translate their findings into a focused proof of concept that makes a possible future workflow visible, testable, and open to critique.

The questions

The course asks not only what AI can automate, but where it can meaningfully assist architectural work, what knowledge should remain legible, who benefits from its introduction, and how the architect's role may change.
