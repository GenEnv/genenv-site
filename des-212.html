DES 212 Design Media II

Designing rules, not just forms.

DES 212 Design Media II introduces computational thinking as a generative medium for design. Moving beyond the use of digital tools to document finished ideas, the course asks students to design relationships, rules, and feedback systems that can produce and transform form. Rhino and Grasshopper provide the primary working environment, connecting technical precision with a broader understanding of how computational tools influence design decisions.

Structure

The course moves through three interconnected areas: the logic of form, the intelligence of matter, and advanced form-finding. Students connect parametric systems with AI-assisted workflows, digital fabrication, spatial data, environmental simulation, and evolutionary design. Lectures, technical tutorials, and project-based assignments develop both computational fluency and the ability to communicate how a design was generated, evaluated, and revised.

Authorship

Throughout the course, students are encouraged to treat emerging technologies as collaborators while remaining responsible for authorship, technical decisions, and the consequences of generated results.
