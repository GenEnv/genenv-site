Human-AI Co-Design

Bringing performance into the generative design process.

Human-AI Co-Design investigates how agentic AI can become a reliable, performance-aware partner in early-stage design. The framework interprets design intent from natural language, sketches, and three-dimensional models, then connects generative design with feedback on energy, carbon, structural feasibility, and regulatory compliance. Instead of producing visually convincing but technically disconnected outputs, it creates live and editable design proposals that can be evaluated, revised, and traced throughout the design process.

The gap

In the architecture, engineering, and construction industry, critical performance checks often happen after major design decisions have been made, resulting in costly revisions and missed opportunities. This research brings performance analysis into the generative design loop and grounds AI reasoning in Canadian building codes, climate data, and sustainability guidelines. It aims to make AI-assisted design more useful, explainable, and verifiable while preserving human judgment.

Current stage

This work-in-progress research program is supported by the Natural Sciences and Engineering Research Council of Canada (NSERC). The current stage focuses on developing the core agentic framework, connecting design and analysis tools, and establishing verification methods for AI-generated proposals.
