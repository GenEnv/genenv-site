PhD positions · Fully funded

PhD positions are fully funded and center on the lab's funded research projects: developing agentic AI systems that interpret regulations and design intent, generate editable and verifiable proposals, and connect generative design with real performance feedback.

Social Sciences and Humanities Research Council (SSHRC)

Decoding Urban Form

Making zoning visible, testable, and open to more people — agentic AI that translates zoning regulations into accessible spatial knowledge and site-specific 3D massing proposals.

Natural Sciences and Engineering Research Council of Canada (NSERC)

Human-AI Co-Design

Bringing performance into the generative design process — a performance-aware agentic framework that connects generative design with feedback on energy, carbon, structural feasibility, and regulatory compliance. The PhD student will lead the development of its agentic AI framework and verification protocols.

Master's students · MASA / MASLA

The lab also welcomes master's students in UBC SALA's Master of Advanced Studies in Architecture (MASA) and Master of Advanced Studies in Landscape Architecture (MASLA) programs to develop research aligned with the lab's directions.

Collaborations

Beyond formal positions, the lab is interested in other types of collaboration and contribution. Researchers, practitioners, and students who want to work on the lab's projects and tools — in any capacity — are welcome to reach out.
