Join GenEnv

The Generative Environments Lab (GenEnv) welcomes students and collaborators interested in artificial intelligence, computational design, and interdisciplinary research on the built environment.

Fully funded PhD opportunities

GenEnv is recruiting PhD students for September 2027 admission through SALA's PhD in Design, Technology and Society program. Selected PhD students will receive funding for four years, with the specific package confirmed in the official offer of admission.

Research may develop across three interconnected areas:

Human-AI co-design

Developing generative AI methods, interactive systems, and computational workflows that support design exploration and collaboration in architecture, landscape architecture, and urban design.

Performance-informed design

Integrating environmental, material, structural, and other performance considerations into early-stage design processes through AI and computational methods.

Agentic AI for urban knowledge

Developing AI systems that connect planning documents, regulations, spatial data, and design information, making complex urban knowledge more accessible and actionable.

Projects may involve computational prototyping, design research, spatial analysis, interface development, or user studies. Applicants are not expected to work across all three areas.

Applicants may come from architecture, landscape architecture, urban design, planning, engineering, computer science, human-computer interaction, or related fields. Strong candidates should demonstrate research potential and relevant experience in one or more of the following:

  • Computational design, programming, or digital prototyping
  • Generative AI, large language models, or agentic systems
  • Parametric modelling, simulation, GIS, or spatial data analysis
  • Environmental or building performance analysis
  • Interaction design, user research, or participatory methods
  • Design research, academic writing, and research communication

Interdisciplinary backgrounds and approaches are encouraged.

Master's research students

GenEnv also welcomes inquiries from prospective students in SALA's Master of Advanced Studies in Architecture (MASA) and Master of Advanced Studies in Landscape Architecture (MASLA) programs.

Prospective master's students should propose an independent research direction that aligns with GenEnv's research. Supervision depends on research fit, faculty capacity, and the requirements of the relevant SALA program. Funding for master's students is not guaranteed.

Other collaborations and contributions

GenEnv is open to collaborations with UBC students, visiting researchers, designers, developers, community organizations, and academic or industry partners.

Depending on research fit, project needs, capacity, and UBC policies, participation may include research assistantships, thesis or course-based research, visiting collaborations, workshops, short-term projects, or independent project contributions. Not all opportunities are funded, and the scope and arrangement will be discussed before participation begins.

How to express interest

Please email Xun Liu at xliu@sala.ubc.ca and identify the opportunity or research area in the subject line.

Include:

  • A current CV, including your publication record if applicable
  • Relevant work samples, such as a portfolio or writing sample
  • A GitHub or other project repository link, if applicable
  • A cover letter describing:
    • why you are interested in GenEnv
    • your relevant experience and skills
    • the research direction you would like to explore

Review of inquiries and interviews for the 2027 PhD intake will begin in late October 2026. Shortlisted candidates must also complete the official UBC and SALA application process.
