ARCH 515 Design Media I (Landscape Session)

Learning to see through media.

ARCH 515 Design Media I introduces representation as both a practical skill and a way of thinking in landscape architecture. Drawings and models do more than document finished ideas. Decisions about projection, scale, line, composition, and medium determine what becomes visible, comparable, and open to revision.

Structure

Developed in coordination with LARC 501, the course moves across sketching, measured drawing, diagrams, hybrid representation, geometric projection, topographic modeling, physical models, and portfolio composition.

Practice

Students combine manual and digital techniques through an iterative practice in which representation becomes a method for observing, testing, and communicating landscape.
