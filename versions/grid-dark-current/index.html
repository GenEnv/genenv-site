About

Generative Environments Lab studies spatial AI, design media, and computational methods for constructed environments.

Founded by Xun Liu at UBC SALA, the lab connects architecture, landscape architecture, artificial intelligence, and design computation.

The website is an evolving archive of research projects, tools, teaching, exhibitions, publications, and selected student work.
